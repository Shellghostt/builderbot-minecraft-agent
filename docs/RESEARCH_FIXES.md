# Research-backed fixes (Claude, 2026-10-13)

Ranked by expected impact. Sources at the end. Verify each on this server before relying on it.

## 1. Make the soak measurable: use the renamed game rules
- Since Minecraft 1.21.11 (snapshot 25w44a) all game rules were renamed to snake_case registry names, and the old camelCase names are rejected ("Incorrect argument for command"). 26.x inherits this. Mapping we need: `doMobSpawning` -> `spawn_mobs`, `doDaylightCycle` -> `advance_time`, `doWeatherCycle` -> `advance_weather`; 26.x also has `spawn_monsters` as a game rule.
- `tests/soak.py` still sends `gamerule doMobSpawning false` (silently fails; that is why GLM concluded "gamerule unavailable on 26.3"). `tests/integration.py` already uses `spawn_mobs` correctly. Use tab-completion / `gamerule <name>` output on this server to confirm each name.
- Soak "normal" mode: `gamerule advance_time false`, `time set 1000`, `gamerule advance_weather false`, `weather clear` -> deterministic daylight for the whole 10-minute run, so the ladder is measured without night or rain. Query the rules back and fail-closed if any is not set.
- Soak "hard" mode: keep natural time/weather/spawning (realistic survival), but still start at confirmed dawn.
- Restore all rules at the end, as today.

## 2. Dark oak forests are daytime monster zones (confirmed)
- Since 1.18 hostile mobs spawn at block light 0. Dark forest canopy keeps the floor dark enough for zombies, spiders and skeletons in daytime. Skeletons do not burn when sunlight level is 11 or less or with a light-blocking block overhead, and they actively seek shade by day.
- So the "move to open sky before working" rule is correct: before gathering, choose a standing spot with sky light >= 12 (unobstructed sky) near reachable logs (forest edge). Undead burn there in daytime. When unarmed and a skeleton is shooting, move into open sunlight or break line of sight; do not hide under the canopy (that is where the skeletons are).

## 3. Port AltoClef's force field (legit form)
- AltoClef's `MobDefenseChain.doForceField()` runs every tick regardless of the current task: it hits any hostile Monster within reach that can see the player (`LookHelper.seesPlayer(entity, player, 10)`). Our port explicitly skipped it ("a server would see the swings").
- On our own servers, hitting a hostile that walks up to you while you chop is normal play. Port a legit version: each tick, if a hostile that targets/sees the bot is within melee reach (<= 3 blocks) and the attack cooldown is full, look at it at human speed and swing with the best weapon (sword > axe > pickaxe > empty hand; never while holding a block or food; switch hotbar first). Do not interrupt eating or placing; do not attack neutral mobs or players. This lets gathering continue while zombies are dealt with, exactly as AltoClef does.

## 4. Use the pathfinder's own mob avoidance
- mineflayer-pathfinder 2.4.5 `Movements` has `entitiesToAvoid` (Set of registry names), `entityCost`, `allowEntityDetection`, and the `exclusionAreasStep` cost hooks. We use none of the entity ones.
- While unarmed or low health: add `creeper` to `entitiesToAvoid`; add an `exclusionAreasStep` cost that grows inside ~8 blocks of any hostile (and along a skeleton's line of sight), refreshed on each replan. Keep a stuck/no-progress watchdog: known pathfinder issues #222 and #273 hang silently on partial paths and on blocks it won't break (e.g. leaves without a hoe), without firing events.

## 5. Creeper damage (still ~16.8 in the arena)
- The fuse starts within ~3 blocks and fires after 1.5 s, but only while the creeper keeps line of sight; getting ~7 blocks away or breaking line of sight cancels it. Normal blast power 3, charged 6.
- Expert play: bow first; otherwise hit once with knockback and back off immediately (repeat), never stand within 3 blocks. Detect fusing from entity metadata (swelling) and treat it as top priority: break line of sight or get past 7 blocks. Find which arena step lets it reach fuse range.

## 6. 26.3 protocol support (watch, do not switch yet)
- Upstream mineflayer: PR #4162 (tick-boundary test allowing tick_end between movement and pong) merged on Oct 10; #4128 (tick_end per physics tick), #4137 (tick_end every client tick 1.21.2+), #4140 (player_loaded after chunk), #4130 (26.3 teleport confirm) were open at last check; issue #4160 (sneak via player_input on 1.21.3-1.21.5).
- A third-party fork (Misty4119/mineflayer) claims "complete Minecraft 26.3 support" merged in that fork on Oct 10. Unverified and unofficial: do not install it. When upstream ships a release with these PRs, test it against our compat.js workarounds and drop the hacks it makes redundant; native 26.3 would also remove ViaBackwards from the loop.

## 7. Smaller items
- mineflayer-collectblock supports `ignoreNoPath` and is wrapped in try/catch + timeouts in Voyager/ADAM-derived code; mirror the pattern: cap retries per target, blacklist, move to the next tree.
- Leaves: pathfinder issue #222 notes it will not break leaves without a hoe; make sure our dig rules allow hand-breaking leaves for canopy escape (already partly done) and test it.

## Update 2026-10-13 22:20 (Claude, after soak-results-20261013-221428)
- First wooden pickaxe in a valid soak: normal1 at 90 s (table at 60 s). The other 5 runs: none.
- **Table placement is still the main blocker** (`src/worker.js` was last changed at 18:47, before CURSOR_NEXT.md item 1). hard3 had 14 dark-oak planks, 4 sticks and a table, then failed `craft wooden_pickaxe` 8x with "No clear spot to place a crafting_table". `placeBlockNear` only tries the TOP face of the ground in 12 fixed cells and needs 2 air cells there, which is rare in a dense dark-oak floor (trunks, saplings, mushrooms, ferns).
- How Voyager/ADAM solve it (`placeItem`): pick a target cell, then try all 6 neighbours as the reference block (any non-air, solid neighbour; `faceVectors` up/down/N/S/E/W) and place against that face, pathfinding to a placement goal first. Port that: widen the candidate cells to a radius of ~3 around the bot (any y from feet-1 to feet+1), accept a cell that is air (only 1 cell needed for a table: the bot does not stand in it), use any solid neighbour face as the reference, skip replaceable references (snow layer, short/tall grass, fern, dead bush, vines), and as a last resort break one natural soft block (leaves, short grass, dirt) next to the bot to make room. Source: https://huggingface.co/OpenCausaLab/ADAM/blob/main/Adam/control_primitives_context/placeItem.js
- normal2/3: 0 logs. "Navigation left the surface at (349, 172, -51)" + "nearby trunks could not be reached": the normal anchor's spruce grove is on steep terrain; consider a flatter normal anchor (scout for >= 5 trunks within 12 blocks, all with bases within +-3 y of the stand).

## Update 2026-10-14 07:10 (Claude): bow aim
Combat run 73186: 3 arrows fired at a creeper, 0 hits ("ViaVersion ballistics still miss"). Proven solutions exist for mineflayer bow aim with drag-aware ballistics and target-motion prediction:
- `minecrafthawkeye` (npm): parabolic trajectory with air friction, auto-aim/one-shot API, claims reliable hits at range; also used by many mineflayer PvP bots. https://npmjs.com/package/minecrafthawkeye
- `mineflayer-projectile` (npm): optimal firing angle, target velocity/acceleration prediction, block intersection. https://npmjs.org/package/mineflayer-projectile
Prefer porting the aim math (gravity 0.05/tick, drag 0.99/tick for arrows, launch speed 3 at full draw, lead the target by its velocity x flight ticks) into `src/combat.js` over adding a dependency; if adding one, justify it in the handover (AGENTS rule). Only on the user's own servers. Verify full draw (>= 20 ticks / 1 s) before release: a 0.27 s release is a weak, short shot.
